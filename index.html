Radiate Wellness: Live Client Consultancy
September to December 2023 · Financial Lead (Team of 5) · Excel
+

The Client

Radiate Wellness is a Canadian nutrition and wellness company owned by Sandra Millward, a registered nutritionist. The company offers personalised diet plans, vitamin protocols, and lifestyle programs. Our team was asked to explore the viability of a subscription based app and provide financial analysis to support the decision.

The Problem I Found

When I started analysing the income statement, the numbers did not make sense. Operating expenses consistently exceeded revenue by two to three times, yet the business was still functioning. After digging deeper and organising a second client meeting, I discovered that expenses from the owner's other unrelated businesses had been mixed into Radiate Wellness's accounts. The "other expenses" category alone was $23,850 in 2018, nearly matching the entire revenue of $30,364.

I raised this directly with the client. It was not a comfortable conversation, but ignoring it would have made every recommendation we gave unreliable. Once the data was corrected, the financial picture changed entirely.

What I Delivered

Built a three statement model (Income Statement, Balance Sheet, Cash Flow Statement) from six years of data with automated ratio analysis using the DuPont framework, liquidity ratios, and solvency ratios. All statements cross linked.

Based on the corrected financials and the 75% revenue decline, I shifted the team's recommendations from expansion to cost management and subscription based pricing. The client adopted this direction.

Coordinated five team members across UK and Canada time zones with weekly milestones and twice weekly check ins over a four month engagement. All deliverables completed on schedule.

What this taught me: the most important thing I did was not the model. It was catching an error everyone else had missed and having the confidence to raise it with the client. That instinct, verifying before trusting and escalating when something looks wrong, is exactly what fund accounting demands.

Short Run: Market Reaction

I built an Excel based market model using Bloomberg price data for Informa and the FTSE 100 across a 200 day estimation window. Applied OLS regression (R² = 0.467, slope = 0.747) to estimate expected returns, then calculated abnormal returns around the announcement date.
