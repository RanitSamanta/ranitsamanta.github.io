Radiate Wellness · Sep–Dec 2023 · Financial Lead (Team of 5) · Excel

Live Client Consultancy

I found expenses in the accounts that did not belong there, and raised it with the client.

The client

Radiate Wellness is a Canadian nutrition company. Our team was asked to explore the viability of a subscription-based app and provide the financial analysis behind the decision.

The problem I found

Operating expenses consistently exceeded revenue by two to three times, yet the business kept running. After a second client meeting, I discovered expenses from the owner's unrelated businesses had been mixed into the accounts. "Other expenses" alone came to $23,850 in 2018, against total revenue of $30,364.

I raised it directly. Not a comfortable conversation, but ignoring it would have made every recommendation unreliable.
